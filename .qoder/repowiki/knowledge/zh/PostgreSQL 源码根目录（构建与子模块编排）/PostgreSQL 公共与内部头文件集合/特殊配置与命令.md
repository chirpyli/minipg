运行 `make` 会首先生成 pg_config.h、pg_config_ext.h、pg_config_os.h；`make install` 会将整个 src/include 树拷贝到 $includedir_server 及 $includedir（公共接口），并在 vpath_build=yes 时额外复制 parser/gram.h、catalog/schemapg.h、catalog/pg_*_d.h、utils/probes.h、storage/lwlocknames.h 等生成头；`make clean` 仅删除 utils/fmgroids.h、utils/fmgrprotos.h、utils/errcodes.h、utils/header-stamp、parser/gram.h、storage/lwlocknames.h、utils/probes.h、catalog/schemapg.h、catalog/system_fk_info.h、catalog/pg_*_d.h、catalog/header-stamp；`make distclean/maintainer-clean` 还会删除 pg_config*.h 与 stamp-h/stamp-ext-h。
